## Manual image-relevance checklist

- Confirm the first (cover) photo actually represents the advertised item or service.
- Check all other images belong to the listing, are permitted by campus rules, and are not misleading.
- Confirm the title explains what a buyer will actually receive and the price basis is comprehensible.
- Contact the seller to improve unclear descriptions and photos; do not silently replace seller content.
- If an already published item may violate policy, follow the existing report and moderation workflow. This audit did not change publication state.

Image subject-to-title relevance cannot be reliably established from metadata alone; human review is still required.
